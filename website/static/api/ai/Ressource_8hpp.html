Detailed Description

Resource types and elevation (upgrade) cost tables for the Zappy AI.

Defines the stones and food that exist on the map, the inventory a player carries, and the resource cost required to elevate from one level to the next.

enum Ressource

The kinds of resources that can be found on the map.

The six stones are used for elevation rituals, while FOOD is consumed to keep the player alive.

Elevation cost table indexed by current level minus one.

Entry upgradeCosts[i] holds the stones needed to elevate from level i + 1 to level i + 2 (e.g. upgradeCosts[0] is the 1->2 cost and upgradeCosts[6] is the 7->8 cost).
